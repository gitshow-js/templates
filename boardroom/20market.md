# Segment Performance

Revenue by operating segment, FY26 versus FY25, constant currency:

| Segment | FY25 | FY26 | Growth | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial Solutions | 612.4 | 689.1 | <span class="plus">+12.5%</span> | 18.2% |
| Connected Services | 244.8 | 318.6 | <span class="plus">+30.1%</span> | 22.7% |
| Legacy Hardware | 198.3 | 171.0 | <span class="minus">−13.8%</span> | 9.4% |
| Group total | 1,055.5 | 1,178.7 | <span class="plus">+11.7%</span> | 17.9% |

<div class="col">
<div class="success">
**Connected Services** is now <em>27%</em> of revenue, up from 23%, and carries the group's richest margin.
</div>
</div>
<div class="col">
<div class="warning">
**Legacy Hardware** continues its managed decline. We expect it below 10% of revenue by FY28.
</div>
</div>
