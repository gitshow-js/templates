# Three Strategic Priorities

<p class="lede">For FY27 we are concentrating capital and attention on three bets &mdash; no more.</p>

## 01 — Scale Connected Services
<div class="success">
Double recurring revenue to **€500m by FY28**. Fund the platform build and a focused sales expansion in DACH and the Nordics.
</div>

## 02 — Wind Down Legacy Hardware
<div class="warning">
Migrate the installed base onto subscription. Accept a **€40m revenue headwind** in FY27 to remove a margin drag and free up working capital.
</div>

## 03 — Disciplined Capital Return
<div class="callout">
Maintain net leverage **below 1.5×**, fund the dividend, and authorise a **€60m** buyback only after the platform investment is committed.
</div>

Note:
The priorities are sequenced, not parallel. Connected Services is the growth
engine; the Legacy wind-down funds it; capital return is the reward for
discipline, deliberately last. Press the board on whether they agree with the
ordering.
