<div class="col">

## The pieces
- A <span class="keyterm">hash function</span> turns a key into a slot
- A <span class="keyterm">hash table</span> stores items by their slot
- A <span class="keyterm">collision</span> is two keys, one slot &ndash; fixed by **chaining**

</div>
<div class="col">

## Why it matters
- Lookups feel like **one** step, not a search
- Same key &rarr; same slot, every single time
- It is how dictionaries, sets, and caches work under the hood

</div>
