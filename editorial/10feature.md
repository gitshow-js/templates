# The Opening

<span class="kicker">Feature · The Slow Web</span>

<p class="dropcap">There is a particular silence to a page that has finished loading and asks nothing more of you. No autoplay, no infinite scroll, no quiet tug toward the next thing. For a generation raised on the feed, that silence reads first as a bug — and then, slowly, as a kind of <em>luxury</em>. The slow web is not nostalgia. It is a design stance: that attention is the scarcest material we work in, and it deserves to be spent, not harvested.</p>

<p>What follows is a working argument, drawn from three studios who chose restraint on purpose — and measured what they got back.</p>
