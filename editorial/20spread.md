## What the feed optimises
The feed is tuned for a single verb: <em>more</em>. More sessions, more dwell, more return. Every interface decision compounds toward it.

- Endless surfaces, no natural edges
- Notifications as a default, not a choice
- Engagement counted; satisfaction guessed

<div class="callout">
A metric you can measure quietly becomes the metric you serve.
</div>

</div>
<div class="col">

## What craft optimises
A made thing is tuned for a different verb: <em>finish</em>. It has an ending, and trusts you to reach it.

- Pages with edges you can feel
- Defaults that respect the reader's time
- Fewer features, chosen on purpose

<div class="success">
Three studios shipped <strong>less</strong> and saw readers stay <strong>longer</strong>.
</div>

</div>
